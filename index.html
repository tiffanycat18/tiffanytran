The Beautiful Melancholy of Being a Woman is a self-portrait project that documents me looking at myself unapologetically, sitting with the sorrow, paradoxes, and vulnerability of being a woman, and learning what it means to be seen.

Tiffany Tran is a multidisciplinary designer, filmmaker, photographer, and visual storyteller interested in fashion, editorial, and cinematic storytelling. Working across film, photography, graphic design, and digital experiences, she creates thoughtful and experimental work that explores memory, emotion, identity, connection, and what it means to be human.
